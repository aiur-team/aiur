**Complexity:** 3
**Phase hint:** 5
**Area:** area:daemon
**Depends on:**

- SD-401 and SD-402: NowRows counts replace the grid totals.
- SD-403: `Build.Usage` meters replace `StreamdeckProjection.Meters`.
- SD-303 and SD-304: the `Conversation.History` chat and turns replace `StreamdeckLogs`.
- SD-103 and SD-104: no renderer reads the old TS models or the emulator descriptors.

**Serializes with:** SD-202, SD-401, SD-403 (`stream_deck_grid.ex`, `streamdeck_projection.ex`, `streamdeck_channel.ex`, `surface.ts`)

## Outcome
The deck has no data logic of its own. Every number it shows comes from a shared read model
through `AiurWeb.Streamdeck.Deck`. The listed deck-only modules, their TS twins, the old TS drawing
stack and their tests are deleted.

`evidence/A-findings.md` §8 lists every production caller and every test for each target.
