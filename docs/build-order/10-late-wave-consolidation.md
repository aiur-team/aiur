## Why (measured, this run)

Merging is not the bottleneck — merged PRs run a **1.7h median** open→merge (1/58 over 24h).
The cost is *unfinished* PRs and repeated per-ticket ceremony:

- **8/12** long-stalled PRs had **no live agent** (8/8 of those tickets carry `agent:paused`/`agent:error`).
- 4 PRs spun review→rework **25–40×** (88–98 `agent:*` transitions each).
- #1091 alone: **85 cold dispatches / ~420M quota-units**, though its feature was done in **53 minutes** —
  the rest was the acceptance loop (20% of dispatches productive; 67% produced no commit).

Consolidation targets the *repeated ceremony*, not the engineering: 23 independent review/CI/
rebase tails become 5.
