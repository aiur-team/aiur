## Non-happy paths

- **Registered names:** `Aiur.Tmux`, `AttachPool` and per-slot names via
  `SlotRegistry` must not change. If `Tmux.Server` used `__MODULE__` for the name, the
  30 callers would fail with `:noproc`.
- **Paint wait is a timing loop:** `retry_wait_for_paint` retries with sleeps; moving
  it must keep the retry count and delay. `warm_attach_open_test.exs:45-71` guards the
  pattern; repoint it, do not drop it.
- **Slot crash and restart:** `Slot` registers in `SlotRegistry` in `init`
  (`:169`) and `AttachPool` reacts to `{:slot_terminated, …}` (`:266`). No change in
  this flow; the moved code holds no process state of its own.
- **Async tests:** `app_test.exs` stays `async: false` (mocks registered by name);
  renderer and pane-manager splits keep `async: true`.
- **Coverage:** see the blocker above.
