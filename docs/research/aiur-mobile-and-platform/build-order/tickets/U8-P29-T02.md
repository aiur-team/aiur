  dashboard snapshot, control-center Units) is the single most cited owner in U6 and

## Non-happy paths

- **Timeouts and unavailable:** `status_api_call/4` maps `:exit` to `:timeout` or
  `:unavailable` (`distinguish_timeout?`). It stays in `StatusReport`; do not collapse
  the two (AGENTS.md "collapsed cause" rule).
- **Unknown progress:** `retained_activity_entry/1` returns `status: :unknown` when no
  reading exists, and the snapshot keeps a stale reading instead of zero. Move these
  clauses intact; the tests at `orchestrator_status_test.exs:2503-2561` and
  `status_report_test.exs:374-440` guard them.
- **Clock reads:** `snapshot_payload/1` reads `DateTime.utc_now()` and
  `System.monotonic_time/1` once at the top and passes them down. Keep one read; do not
  let each sub-module read its own clock.
- **Async test isolation:** `status_report_test.exs` is `async: true`; the new split
  files keep it. `orchestrator_status_test` stays synchronous.
- **Coverage:** `StatusReport` is not in `ignore_modules`; the moved lines keep their
  current coverage.
