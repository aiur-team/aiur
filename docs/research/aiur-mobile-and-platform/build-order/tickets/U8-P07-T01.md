or a value. Timer starts that call `Process.send_after(self(), …)` stay in the GenServer
or take `pid` explicitly. Rationale: same pattern as the six existing siblings; the
GenServer keeps every side effect.

and the rest. `github_graph_test.exs` → `github_graph/catalog_test.exs`,

`shared_resource_store_test.exs` → move "a newer body is never overwritten" and "failing open"
