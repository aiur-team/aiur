## Non-happy paths

- `AgentQueueStore` transitions are pure functions on a struct persisted elsewhere. Keep
  the struct definition in `agent_queue_store.ex`; `Correlation` takes and returns it.
- `QueueDrain.wait_for_operator_message` blocks in the runner process. Keep it in
  `QueueDrain`.
- Startup failure paths decide whether a provider may still be running
  (`mark_provider_cleanup_unknown`). Moving them must keep the "unknown" outcome; do not
  default it to "cleaned".
- App-server tests spawn ports with fake binaries; keep each file's `setup` that writes
  the fake script and its `on_exit` cleanup. Keep `async:` settings as they are.
