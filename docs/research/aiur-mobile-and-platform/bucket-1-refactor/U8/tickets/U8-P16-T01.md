## Chosen design

- **Shape:** `Aiur.DecisionStore` keeps the client API, `init/1`, every `handle_call`,
  `handle_continue`, `handle_info` and `terminate`. Each `handle_call` body becomes one
  call into a pure module: `Module.handle(state, args) :: {reply, state}`. Pure modules
  never call `GenServer`, never send to `self()` except through a returned action list
  the GenServer executes, and never hold state.

## Non-happy paths

- **Ambiguous fsync and projection failure:** U6's matrix decides the behavior; the move
  must keep today's code path exactly. The "restart recovery", "corruption" and "repair
  mode" tests guard it.
- **Task and monitor ownership:** `Process.monitor`, `Task.Supervisor.async_nolink` and
  `Process.send_after` must stay in the GenServer process. A pure module that calls them
  from inside a `handle_call` still runs in the GenServer; that is allowed, but it must
  not spawn a new process that then owns the monitor.
- **Restart replay:** the GenServer restarts under its supervisor and replays the
  journal. No new module may hold ETS or `persistent_term` state.
- **Stale reads:** `retained_*` readers go through `decision_query/store_reader.ex`;
  keep that path.
