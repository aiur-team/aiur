# Feature synthesis checkpoint

The five frozen inventories contain 216 entries. Every one of the 91 raw
cut/merge/externalize proposals has a skeptical challenge. The reconciled
working decisions, original evidence and complete challenge notes are in
`features.json`; the five surface tables are in `usage-matrix.md` and `matrix/`.

`feature-inventory.md` explains decision boundaries and inventory omissions.
`loc-reduction.md` distinguishes conditional whole-file removal footprints
from implemented net savings. Re-run `tooling/feature_synthesis.py` and
`tooling/feature_loc_scenarios.py <complete-snapshot>` to reproduce the tables.

These are frozen `3339b887` planning judgments. They have not been applied to
the product, measured as a post-change reduction, or validated against every
later source change.
